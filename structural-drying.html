Professional Structural Drying Services

Professional drying of walls, floors, ceilings and framing to help prevent additional damage after water intrusion.

Call for 24/7 Emergency Service Request an Estimate

Why Structural Drying Matters

Moisture trapped inside building materials can lead to warping, mold growth and long-term structural problems if it isn't properly dried and monitored.

Our Drying Process

  • Water extraction
  • Air movement
  • Dehumidification
  • Moisture monitoring

Walls, Floors & Framing

Walls & Drywall

Targeted drying to protect drywall and framing behind the surface.

Floor Systems & Framing

Drying subfloors, joists and structural framing to prevent warping and rot.

Commercial Drying & Reconstruction

We provide structural drying for commercial properties of all sizes, and can move directly into reconstruction once materials are fully dry.
