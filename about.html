Our Story

One Company, From Emergency Response to Reconstruction

PMC Restoration was built to solve a common problem for property owners: needing one company to clean up damage and a separate company to rebuild it. We manage the full recovery process — emergency response, water extraction, drying, demolition, cleanup, repairs, finishing and complete reconstruction — under one roof.

We work with homeowners, commercial property owners, property managers, contractors and restoration partners throughout Greater Boston and Massachusetts.
